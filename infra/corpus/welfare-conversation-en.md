lang: en
reviewed_by: team
version: 1
---

A welfare conversation is a practical talk with an officer about rest, leave, pay, family, or strain. It is not a punishment parade. You can ask for time, a referral, or help with paperwork. You can also say you do not want to talk today. Saathi can offer contact when you ask.
