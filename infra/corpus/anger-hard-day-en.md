title: Anger after a hard day
lang: en
reviewed_by: team
version: 1
---

Anger after a long duty is common. Step away if you can. Cool water on the wrists, a slow walk around the block, or writing one sentence in your journal can take the heat down. Do not take it out on family on a call. A counsellor conversation is available if anger keeps coming back.
