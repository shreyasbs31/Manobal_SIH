title: Box breathing
lang: en
reviewed_by: team

---

Sit with both feet on the floor. Breathe in for four, hold for four, out for four, hold for four. Repeat four rounds. If you feel dizzy, stop and breathe normally. This is a way to settle the body, not a treatment. The toolkit has a guided ring if you prefer audio.
