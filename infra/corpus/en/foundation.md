---

After a long duty period, find a quiet place if one is available. Sit with
both feet supported and let your shoulders soften. Breathe in gently for four
counts and breathe out for six counts. Repeat for one minute.

This is a small recovery step. It does not replace rest, leave, or a
conversation with a person you trust.
