title: Sleep on rotating shifts
lang: en
reviewed_by: team
version: 1
---

Rotating shifts can leave sleep feeling broken. Protect a dark, quiet block after night duty, even if it is shorter than a full night. Tell your roommate you are sleeping. Avoid bright screens in the last twenty minutes. If sleep will not come, get up, sit quietly, and try again. Saathi can open a sleep planner. A welfare officer can help if roster rest keeps slipping.
