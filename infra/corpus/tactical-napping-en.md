title: Tactical napping
lang: en
reviewed_by: team
version: 1
---

A short nap of ten to twenty minutes can take the edge off before a long stretch of duty. Set an alarm. Sit or lie somewhere you will be found if needed. After the nap, stand, drink water, and give yourself a few minutes before you start work. Naps do not replace a rest day.
