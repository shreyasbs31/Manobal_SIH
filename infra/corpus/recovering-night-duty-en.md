title: Recovering after night duty
lang: en
reviewed_by: team
version: 1
---

After a night stretch, eat something simple, dim the light, and delay heavy talk if you can. Caffeine late in the morning can steal the next sleep. If you feel low or snappy, that can be tiredness. Ask for a quiet hour. If this pattern lasts many days, tell someone you trust or use check-in.
